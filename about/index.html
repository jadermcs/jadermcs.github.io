about

During my academic career, I was exposed to many theories that at first seemed impossible for me to grasp, but through persistence or luck I ended up finding good analogies along the way that turned something challenging into something trivial. This process made me believe that no individual is incapable of understanding a new idea; the only limitation is having a good analogy for it. Not only in the scientific field, but also in debates and internet forums, I see many conflicts arise because the participants don't really understand each other's analogies.

In this blog, I will try to do the same for concepts, theories, and opinions I've been studying for the last decade. The idea is to create good analogies for topics in many subjects: computer science, philosophy, economics, etc. You may disagree with an idea, but if the analogy I propose makes you fully understand the complexity and scope of the argument, I will consider it a success.

Jader Martins - 2021
